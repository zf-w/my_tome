# Friday, January 30th

I have been thinking about altering the "mdtome" structure. Previously, I have been using filenames to embed "sequence", "sub-tome" tree type, URL, and title for navigation. Today, I have tried to use CSV files in each "subtree" to represent that information, and I found my previous design was indeed quite helpful. The "navigation title" helped me to avoid having multiple files sharing the same filename. It's just that my previous approach of using a semicolon to separate that information is a bit weird. I guess changing that into "-" might make filenames look a little bit better.

# Wednesday, January 21st

In the afternoon, I tried to use my cast-iron skillet to cook the steak and use the ChefIq cooker to cook the Dojin mushroom sauce. At this time, I bought whole baby bella mushrooms. It's quite interesting to think about how to cut them evenly to the same shape. Learning from my previous purchase of the baby bella mushroom slices, cutting them vertically should be fine, but I tried to cut them from the "center" to make them all in the same shape. I think the meal tasted pretty good.

Before cooking the steak, I tested the stove top a bit, trying to set it to a power similar to the ChefIq cooker: rising to 210 degrees Fahrenheit in 5 minutes. The resulting steak tasted similar, and I'm grateful for that.

In the evening, I picked up a bread key ring on the road and posted a "Found" post on university Reddit in hope of finding the person who lost it.

I tried to cook the Dojin Mustard Steak for the second time. This time, I bought enough mushrooms and shallots. The first try was also quite successful, even though the sauce was a little bit bitter. I have also adjusted the sequence of combining steak and sauce, and poured the sauce first this time. It looked pretty good. I'm grateful.
